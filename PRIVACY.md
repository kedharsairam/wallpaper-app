# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 25, 2026**

## Overview

WallKraft does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Your privacy is built into the design.

## Data Collection

WallKraft collects no personal data. The app does not:

- Create user accounts
- Track your activity
- Use analytics services
- Send crash reports
- Access your contacts, camera, microphone, or location
- Share any data with third parties

## Third-Party Services

WallKraft fetches wallpapers from a third-party API. When you search or browse, your search query and IP address are sent to the API servers as part of the standard network request. The third-party service's own privacy policy applies to those interactions.

WallKraft does not control what data the third-party service collects.

## Local Storage

Wallpapers you choose to download are saved to your device's local storage. These files remain on your device until you delete them. WallKraft has no access to or knowledge of any other files on your device.

## Permissions

WallKraft requests the **Internet** permission to fetch wallpapers. No other permissions are required or requested.

## Children's Privacy

WallKraft does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top will be revised.
