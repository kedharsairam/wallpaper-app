## Full Description

WallKraft lets you browse and download high-quality wallpapers from the Wallhaven community. Discover millions of wallpapers across every category — from breathtaking landscapes and futuristic sci-fi to minimal art and anime.

**Features:**

• Browse thousands of wallpapers across General, Anime, and People categories
• Search by keywords to find exactly what you're looking for
• Filter by date added, relevance, popularity, and top lists
• View wallpaper details: resolution, file size, aspect ratio, tags, and favorites
• Pinch-to-zoom on any wallpaper for a closer look
• Download wallpapers in full resolution directly to your device
• Share wallpapers with friends
• Infinite scroll — keep discovering
• Dark theme, optimized for AMOLED displays
• Material Design 3

**Perfect for:**
Desktop background hunters • Mobile wallpaper collectors • Design inspiration • Digital art enthusiasts

WallKraft uses the Wallhaven API but is not affiliated with or endorsed by Wallhaven.
